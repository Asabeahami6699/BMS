Setting up a new tenant

How a platform administrator registers a company, and how that company’s admin finishes branches, staff, products, and customer SMS.

Banking Management System
For cooperatives and microfinance institutions
October 2026

1. Who does what

A tenant is one company on aabanker. Each company has its own customers, branches, staff, and settings. Two people set a company up.

PersonSigns in asDoes this
Platform super adminPlatform workspaceRegisters the company, chooses products and add-ons, and creates the company admin login.
Company adminThe company workspaceSets the profile, branches, account numbers, staff, bank products, and SMS.

Use a separate company name and a separate SMS sender for every tenant. Customers of Proto Susu must not receive messages headed Evans Susu.

2. Before you start

  • The legal company name, and a short Company ID you will not change later. Example: name Proto Susu, ID proto-susu.
  • Which departments the company paid for: Susu Management, Agency Banking, Loans, Treasury, Investment Management.
  • Which add-ons they paid for. SMS is a separate add-on. It does not unlock a department.
  • The company admin’s full name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters.
  • Branch names and short codes.
  • A numeric account-number prefix shorter than 12 digits.
  • If they want deposit and withdrawal texts: an Arkesel sender name of at most 11 characters, approved on the platform Arkesel account.

3. Register the company

Sign in as the platform super admin. Open Companies.

  1. Click + Register company.
  2. Company ID. A short permanent code, such as proto-susu. Use letters, numbers, and hyphens. Do not reuse another company’s ID.
  3. Company Name. The name staff and customers should recognise, such as Proto Susu.
  4. Subscription. Leave this on active. Inactive companies cannot sign in.
  5. Product modules. Tick only what this company subscribed to. At least one product is required.
  6. Add-ons. Tick paid extras now, or add them later with Edit add-ons.
  7. Click Register company. Copy the registration details and keep them with the company file.

If a menu says Product not enabled, that department was not ticked for this company. SMS does not turn a department on. Open the company row, click Edit products, tick the department, and ask the company admin to sign in again.

4. Create the company admin

Still on Companies, click + Create company admin. You can also use Add admin on that company’s row if you need a second admin later.

  1. Select the company.
  2. Enter the admin’s full name.
  3. Enter the email they will use to sign in.
  4. Enter a password of at least 8 characters.
  5. Click Create company admin.
  6. Copy the login panel and send it to the admin on a private channel. It shows the company, company ID, name, email, and password.
The password is shown once on that success screen. Store it before you close the window.

5. Company admin: first sign-in

The company admin signs in on the aabanker sign-in page with the email and password you created. They land in their company workspace, not the platform Companies page.

Work through Settings in this order.

5.1 Company Profile

Open Settings → Company Profile.

  1. Confirm or correct the company name. This name is also used inside customer SMS.
  2. Enter the phone, email, address, and tax identification number.
  3. Upload a JPEG, PNG, or WebP logo.
  4. Choose the brand colour.
  5. Click Save company profile.

You can open this screen again later, change any field, and save. The sidebar name, logo, and colour update after each save. Removing the logo and saving clears it.

5.2 Branches

Open Settings → Branches. Add every office the company operates.

  1. Enter a short branch code, such as ACC.
  2. Enter the branch name.
  3. Leave the status active.
  4. Save, and repeat for each branch.

If the company has only one branch, aabanker selects that branch automatically on deposit and withdrawal screens. Staff do not see an empty “Select branch” option.

5.3 Account numbers

Open Settings → Account numbers. Customer account numbers are 12 digits. Type the company’s numeric prefix. aabanker adds random digits when a registration is approved. The prefix must be shorter than 12 digits. Save the prefix before the first customer is approved.

5.4 Users and permissions

Open Settings → Users and create each staff member: tellers, field agents, coordinators, customer service, and back office. Assign the branch they work in.

Open Settings → Roles & Permissions when a job needs a duty that is not on by default.

  • A teller records susu deposits with the teller role.
  • To let a teller record a susu withdrawal, tick Record withdrawals. Do not use Open partner bank accounts for that job.
  • After you save a role, that person must sign out and sign in again before the new duty appears.

On a teller deposit, the ledger shows the teller who posted it. It does not add the customer’s field agent to that line. Field-collection batches still show the agent.

5.5 Bank products

Create the savings or partner-bank products the counter will post against. Put the word susu in the name of every susu deposit product, for example Susu Savings. A susu-only company will not see a product that does not contain that word.

6. Customer SMS

Do this only when the SMS Notifications add-on is on the company. One Arkesel account serves the whole platform. Each company still needs its own approved sender name.

6.1 Approve the sender on Arkesel

  1. Sign in to the same Arkesel account that already holds the platform API key. Do not create a second Arkesel account.
  2. Open Sender ID. Do not use Send Bulk SMS to register a name. That page only sends a campaign.
  3. Request a new sender ID. Use the company’s own short name, at most 11 characters, including spaces. Proto Susu is 10 characters.
  4. Paste a purpose of at least 50 characters, for example: Proto Susu sends transactional SMS alerts to its customers when a deposit is credited or a withdrawal is debited on their savings account.
  5. Upload the business certificate if Arkesel asks for it.
  6. Wait until that exact spelling shows as approved. Approval is per name. Evans Susu does not approve Proto Susu.
A bulk campaign is not part of setup. It spends SMS credits. Use it only if Arkesel support asks for a portal test.

6.2 Turn SMS on in aabanker

Sign in as the company admin. Open Settings → Notification Settings.

  1. Turn on Enable customer SMS alerts.
  2. Tick Deposit credited, Withdrawal debited, and Daily susu collection posted for the events this company wants.
  3. Set Sender ID to the approved name, including the space. Example: Proto Susu.
  4. Click Save SMS settings.
  5. Enter your own mobile number and click Send test.

The test is an API SMS. In the Arkesel log, wait until that row says DELIVERED. SUBMITTING or RECHECK means it is still in progress. FAILED means the phone did not get it. Do not keep sending tests. Each test uses a credit. Give Arkesel the message ID and ask why that API SMS failed.

Portal messages and API messages are different. A portal row can be DELIVERED while an API row to the same number is FAILED. aabanker uses the API. The sender must work on API SMS before customers will receive alerts.

6.3 What the customer receives

After a deposit is posted to the ledger, the customer with a valid Ghana number receives a text like this:

A sum of GHS 50.00 has been credited to your Account # **********4563 being deposited by Ama Mensah - GHS 50.00. current balance is: Ghs 1,250.00

A withdrawal uses “debited” and “withdrawal” in the same sentence. The text is sent only after the transaction is posted. Collecting on the field-agent screen does not send a second message. Daily susu sends one text per customer when the batch is posted.

The customer record must have a Ghana mobile number: 024XXXXXXX, 23324XXXXXXX, or 9 digits without the leading zero.

7. Go-live check

  1. Company subscription is active.
  2. Only the paid products and add-ons are ticked.
  3. The company admin can sign in and sign out.
  4. Company Profile is saved.
  5. Every branch exists, and a one-branch company opens with that branch already selected.
  6. The account-number prefix is saved.
  7. A teller can post a deposit. If they should pay withdrawals, Record withdrawals is ticked and they have signed in again.
  8. Susu products contain the word susu.
  9. The Arkesel sender for this company is approved, saved in Notification Settings, and one API test is DELIVERED.
  10. Create one test customer with your phone number, post a small deposit, and confirm the credited SMS and the teller’s name on the ledger.

8. If something fails

SMS settings are missingSMS Notifications is not on this company’s add-ons. Edit add-ons, then sign in again.
